# Miorbi 0.1.1 Beta 4 — Compact notch wings

## 简体中文

按测试反馈撤销下方常显大状态条，恢复紧凑刘海两侧。每侧内容区 48 点，外边距各 6 点：左侧宠物/运行动画，右侧 5h 和 7d 限额上下排列。常显不再显示“运行中”文字，状态详情和控制仅悬停展开。无刘海屏幕继续使用菜单栏面板。

尽量减少占用，但菜单栏非常拥挤时仍可能重叠，不能保证所有设备上零遮挡。macOS 14+。公开测试包为 ad-hoc 签名、未经公证；核对 SHA256SUMS.txt 后，仅对可信应用使用系统设置“仍要打开”，不要关闭全局 Gatekeeper。Apple Music 歌词未实现；网易云歌词和限额同步默认关闭，需各自授权，不承诺零延迟。

## English

Replaces the persistent lower status strip with compact notch wings: 48 points per side plus 6-point margins. Pet/activity animation on the left, stacked five-hour/weekly limits on the right. Full status and controls are available on hover. Unnotched displays retain the menu-bar panel.

Reduces occupancy but cannot guarantee zero overlap on crowded menu bars. macOS 14+. Ad-hoc signed, not notarized; verify SHA256SUMS.txt and use per-app Open Anyway only if trusted. Never disable Gatekeeper globally. Apple Music synced lyrics remain unimplemented; NetEase lyrics and usage sync are off by default and require authorization. Zero latency is not guaranteed.
