# Miorbi 0.1.1 Beta 8 — Completion monitoring

Stop 事件后立即显示“任务结束”，宠物停止跳跃并显示 Zzz；状态与限额保留 5 分钟，Codex 设置可选择 10 分钟。无新任务达到时限后隐藏 Codex 展示并停止闲置用量请求，继续监听新任务；音乐和专注保持可用。新活动立即恢复运行态，其他会话仍运行时优先显示运行。运行中、结束监控中、空闲有明确区分。

After a Stop event, immediately shows Finished and Zzz with no jumping pet. Retains status/limits for 5 minutes, configurable to 10. Hides Codex and stops idle usage requests after the quiet window while listening for new work; music/focus stay available. New activity resumes running; other running sessions retain priority.
